{% blocktrans %}The Dofus Fashionista ("we", "the site") is a free, open-source fan site that automatically optimizes equipment sets for the game Dofus. This page explains what data we collect, why, and the choices you have. We are not affiliated with Ankama Games.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Information we collect" %}

  • {% blocktrans %}Account information. If you create an account, we store your email address and an encrypted password. If you sign in with Google, we store your email address and Google account ID instead of a password: we never see or store your Google password.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans %}Content you create. The characters, projects, builds, tags, comments and shared links you make are stored so the site can provide its features and so you can return to your work.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans %}Messages. If you contact us, we keep the message and the address you provide so we can reply.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans %}Technical data. Like most websites, our servers and content-delivery provider automatically log standard information such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, for security and to keep the service running.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Builds you publish" %}

{% blocktrans %}A build you create while logged in is published the first time it has gear: it then appears in the build gallery, on the pages of the items it wears, and under your display name on your profile, and anyone holding its link can open it. You can turn that off for a build at any time from the build page, and you can untick the box on the creation page before you start. Builds made without an account are never published on their own, and builds created before 11 September 2026 keep the visibility they already had.{% endblocktrans %}
